The motor drivers switch off for 2.5 s first, so the motors go limp: support the arm or rest it in a safe pose. Then J2 and J3 turn back and forth at 25% for about 1 s each, and the claw opens/closes 20°. Keep clear. Press Z at the home pose afterwards, because turning the drivers off clears the arm's zero.
